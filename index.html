Questions from the long grass

Before you ask

Do players need to download anything?

No. Everything runs in the phone's browser. Scan the first QR and you're in.

How big can a crew be?

Anything from a pair to a dozen. Most hunts work best with crews of four to six.

Does it work anywhere?

Anywhere you can draw on a map and get phone signal: parks, high streets, campuses, festival fields, your aunt's enormous garden.

Who judges the photos and videos?

You do, from your phone. Each submission lands in your dashboard and your inbox. Approve it and the crew's next clue appears instantly.

Can you run a hunt for us?

For bigger events, yes. Get in touch and tell us what you're plotting.
