Privacy policy

What we collect

  • Hosts: the hunt you build (title, area, start time, stops, clues, crew names and any player names you type in) and, if you give it, your email address.

  • Payments: handled by Stripe. We receive your email address, the amount and whether the payment succeeded. We never see or store card details. See Stripe's privacy policy.
